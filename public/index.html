About this project

Describe a life situation — someone facing surgery, a Ramadan reflection, a difficult decision, gratitude, or illness — and receive two or three relevant verses from the Qur’an and a short du’a (supplication), rendered as a shareable 1080×1350 image ready for Messenger, WhatsApp, or Instagram.

The suggestion is a starting point — every field is editable before you download. Translations come from widely-recognized English renderings (Sahih International, Yusuf Ali, or Pickthall), with Latin-script transliteration provided as an aid.
